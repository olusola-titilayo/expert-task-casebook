Contractor cash = cost oil + 40% of profit oil - costs spent that year.

The project itself makes 130 (revenue 370 less costs 240). All of that and more goes to the government: 37 of royalty plus 60% of 166.5 of profit oil is 136.9.

**What's surprising:** a profitable project loses money for the investor. The cap means 73.5 of costs are still unrecovered when the contract ends, and they're worth nothing unless the contract provides otherwise. Meanwhile, profit oil is shared every year even though the contractor hasn't recovered its costs.

**What to do:** negotiate the cost cap, seek a longer term so the pool can recover, or get terms for unrecovered costs at expiry. Check also whether the carry-forward survives termination, as that decides whether the 73.5 is really lost.

- Gross revenue by year: 100, 150, 120
- Capex: 180 in year 1. Opex: 20 each year.
- Royalty: 10% of gross revenue
- Cost recovery: capped at 50% of revenue after royalty. Unrecovered costs carry forward.
- Profit oil: contractor 40%, government 60%
- Ignore tax and discounting.

What is the contractor's net cash flow over the contract, and what's the government's take? Comment on anything surprising.

The two streams are stated differently, so they need different rates.

**Operating cash flows are real** (today's money), so they take the real rate. The exact Fisher conversion gives 1.1235 / 1.05 - 1 = **7.00%**:

100/1.07 + 100/1.07^2 + 100/1.07^3 = **$262.43**

**The tax shield is nominal**, because its dollar amount is fixed and inflation erodes it. It takes the nominal rate:

30/1.1235 + 30/1.1235^2 + 30/1.1235^3 = **$71.62**

Total: 262.43 + 71.62 = **$334.06**

Check: growing the operating flows at 5% and discounting at 12.35% gives the same $262.43. The two methods are equivalent as long as they're used consistently.
